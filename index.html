Model notes

This is a schematic, not a reconstruction of actual ground. The bluff, river, and heat pattern are illustrative. No elevation raster, survey, project grading, or water-temperature data is loaded.

For a site-accurate model, replace this terrain with a georeferenced DEM and obtain DOT&PF existing/proposed surface files. The Alaska DGGS Elevation Portal is a starting point for checking elevation-data coverage.
